Plandot · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Summary

Plandot is a personal calendar, to-do and notes app. Your events, tasks, notes, sketches and settings are stored only on your device. We have no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not receive your data.

Who we are

Plandot is published by an individual developer. Contact: zhangwei798879@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (events, tasks, notes, reminders, sketches, repeat rules, history for undo) is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is protected by your device's sandbox and lock. It is not uploaded to us.

The home-screen widget reads a short summary of today's items from the same device storage.

Voice input (optional)

When you tap Start speaking, the app uses your device's speech recognition (Apple Speech on iOS, the system speech service on Android) to turn your words into text. Depending on your device, the audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The app only listens after you tap, and the text stays a draft until you confirm it.

Photos and camera (optional)

When you choose a photo or take one, the app reads its text on the device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android) to build a draft. The image is not stored or uploaded by the app.

On Android, Google ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage metrics (such as device model, OS version and performance data) - not the image or its text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Lock-screen notifications show a generic message by default so event titles are not exposed.

Export

When you export (JSON or .ics), the file is created on your device and handed to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition: only for voice input. Camera and photos: only for reading text from an image you pick. Notifications: only for your reminders. Every feature keeps working if you decline, except the one that needs the permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. If you have a question or request under GDPR, CCPA or other laws, write to us and we will reply within 30 days.

Future online features

Optional online features (shared calendars, an AI assistant, accounts) are not active in this version. Before any of them is turned on, this policy will be updated and the app will ask for your consent before sending anything.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
