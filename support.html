Plandot · Support

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: zhangwei798879@gmail.com. Please include your device model, system version and the app version (Me tab, bottom of the page). We usually reply within 3 business days.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Plandot in system settings. On Android, also allow alarms & reminders, and exclude the app from battery optimisation. Opening the app once re-schedules pending reminders; Me > Reminder status shows what is scheduled.

Back up or move your data

Me > Export my data creates a JSON backup; Export to other calendars creates an .ics file that Apple, Google and Outlook calendars can import.

Delete your data

Delete individual items in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from the device.
